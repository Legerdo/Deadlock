## Kai Morrow — `kai` (player)

- Role: audio restoration technician hired to digitize the archive before demolition. Listens more than she talks.
- Age 24, female. Height ~168 cm, slim-athletic, 7.5 heads.
- Hair: short choppy **black** bob at jaw length, blunt uneven fringe, **one deep-cyan streak at the left temple**.
- Face: sharp attentive dark eyes, faint tired under-eyes, small mole under the right eye, straight determined brows.
- Outfit: **oversized deep-cyan canvas work jacket** with many pockets, left sleeve rolled to the elbow, right sleeve down; coral shoulder patch on the right shoulder with a simple waveform shape (no letters); warm-white high-neck tee; black cargo trousers with one coral strap on the left thigh; chunky black boots with warm-white soles.
- Accessories: **big vintage over-ear headphones (warm white, cyan pads) resting around her neck**; one fingerless black glove on the right hand; tiny cassette-shaped pin on the collar.
- Main colors: CYAN, PAPER, INK, CORAL accents.
- Silhouette: boxy oversized jacket on slim legs, headphone ring at the neck.
- Personality: dry, patient, precise, quietly stubborn.
- Never change: black bob + cyan streak, cyan jacket, headphones at neck, single glove.

- Main colors: INK, SIGNAL, CORAL.
- Silhouette: tall vertical line with a long scarf tail.
- Personality: warm, theatrical, secretive, brave in a reckless way.
- Never change: amber glasses, yellow turtleneck, coral scarf, salt-and-pepper swept hair.
