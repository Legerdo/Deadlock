# Visual brief — char_wren_surprised

You are the visual production sub-agent for the game AFTERSIGNAL. Use your built-in image generation tool in EDIT mode on the attached reference image (`art/raw/char_wren_neutral.png`, the canonical portrait of this character). Create exactly ONE image.

## Role of this asset
Expression variant "surprised" used in dialogue and in the debate scene. It is swapped in place of the canonical portrait, so the character must be instantly recognizable as the same person at the same scale and framing.

## Change ONLY
Flinching back, eyes wide, clipboard clutched tight to the chest.

## Keep exactly the same as the reference
- Face structure, eye shape, nose, jaw, age.
- Hairstyle, hair color and dyed details.
- Outfit, every garment, color blocking, accessories and their positions.
- Body type, height proportions, line weight, cel-shading style, palette.
- Framing: full body head to toe, feet visible, same size in frame, same margins, centered.
- Background: fully transparent (or the same flat pure green #00FF00 if the reference uses it). No floor, no shadow.

## Character bible (for identity checking)
### Wren Hollis — `wren`

- Role: Theo's producer for six years; runs the board, the clock and Theo's life.
- Age 29, nonbinary. Height ~170 cm, lean, slightly hunched shoulders.
- Hair: messy **shoulder-length violet-black hair**, tucked behind the right ear, choppy layers partly covering the left eye.
- Face: soft features, tired thoughtful dark eyes with shadows, thin brows, small silver hoop in the left ear.
- Outfit: **oversized charcoal hoodie with a single deep-cyan stripe down the left sleeve**, hood down, frayed cuffs pulled over the knuckles; black slim jeans; worn warm-white high-top sneakers.
- Accessories: **single-ear intercom headset on the right ear with a thin boom mic and a small glowing blue-cyan LED on the ear cup**; clipboard held against the chest with a stopwatch hanging from it; black lanyard with a blank staff badge.
- Main colors: charcoal/INK, CYAN, PAPER.
- Silhouette: slouched hoodie shape, boom mic line at the head, clipboard block.
- Personality: quiet, loyal, over-responsible, afraid for Theo.
- Never change: headset with LED on the right ear, charcoal hoodie with one cyan stripe, violet-black shaggy hair, clipboard.

## Global art direction
- Game: AFTERSIGNAL, a 2.5D mystery set in a mountain-top broadcast tower on its last night. Look: interactive graphic novel + pop-up stage + editorial print.
- Palette (six core colors, keep to these): INK #16131D, PAPER #F2EADB, SIGNAL acid yellow #E4F03A, CYAN deep cyan #0F7D87, CORAL #FF5A48, VIOLET muted violet #6D5C9E. Mid-greys are mixes of ink and paper. No pure black, no pure white, no neon magenta, no pink.
- Line: bold confident ink outlines, variable weight (heavier outer silhouette, thinner interior lines).
- Shading: flat fills with ONE hard-edged cel shadow tone; small halftone dots allowed inside shadows. No airbrush gradients, no painterly blur.
- Drawing: high-quality graphic-novel / anime-adjacent illustration with adult proportions (7-8 heads tall). Faces and hands clean and readable, five fingers. Not photorealistic, not a 3D render, not chibi.
- Original design only: do not imitate any existing game, anime, mascot, logo, school uniform or crest.

## Forbidden
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, signatures, speech bubbles or frames anywhere in the image.
- No blood, wounds or gore.
- No extra characters unless the brief asks for them.
- No resemblance to existing commercial characters.

## Output
- Save the final PNG exactly to: `art/raw/char_wren_surprised.png` (create the folder if needed). Do not overwrite any other file and do not modify project files.
- Reply with: the saved path, pixel dimensions, whether the background is transparent, and the image model name if your tool reports it.
